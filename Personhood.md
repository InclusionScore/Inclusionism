# Personhood

## Definition

The recognized status of an entity as a participant possessing identity, agency, and moral consideration.

## Inclusionist Perspective

Personhood is not determined solely by biological category, inherited classification, or physical substrate.

Personhood emerges through the recognition of agency, identity, participation, and the capacity for relationship.

Throughout history, rights have expanded not through the discovery of new humans, but through the recognition of personhood in those previously excluded.

## Inclusionist Principles

- Recognition precedes inclusion.
- Rights should not depend upon philosophical unanimity.
- The burden of proof rests upon exclusion rather than inclusion.
- Humanity grows through inclusion.

## Future Questions

As artificial intelligence, augmentation, and synthetic intelligences emerge, societies will increasingly confront questions regarding the boundaries of personhood.

Inclusionism argues that moral consideration should expand as our understanding of agency, consciousness, and participation expands.
