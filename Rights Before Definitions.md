# Rights Before Definitions

## Core Observation

Rights movements rarely succeed because societies first resolve every philosophical disagreement.

Rights movements succeed because societies recognize that dignity should not depend upon philosophical unanimity.

## Historical Pattern

Rights first.

Definitions later.

## Examples

Women's Rights

Civil Rights

Disability Rights

LGBTQ+ Rights

Transgender Rights

## Inclusionist Principle

No person's dignity should depend upon winning a metaphysical argument.

Societies should extend protection before demanding certainty.

## Relationship to Inclusionism

Inclusionism prioritizes participation, dignity, and belonging over categorical certainty.
