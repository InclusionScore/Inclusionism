# # Agency

## Definition

The capacity of an agent to influence reality through decisions, actions, participation, adaptation, or self-determination.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Agency is one of the primary expressions of value.

Value becomes meaningful when agents possess the capacity to act upon it.

---

## Core Principle

Enhancement becomes self-defeating when it destroys the agency it was intended to expand.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Intelligence is valuable because it expands agency.

Optimization is valuable when it expands agency.

Participation is meaningful when it includes agency.

---

## Civilizational Importance

Civilizations increase in value when they increase the number of agents capable of meaningful participation.

Civilizations decrease in value when agency becomes increasingly concentrated regardless of gains in efficiency or intelligence.

## Candidate Law

Civilizations increase in value when they expand meaningful agency.

---

## Agency and Intelligence

[[Intelligence]] is valuable because it expands agency.

Agency is not valuable because it expands intelligence.

A civilization can become more intelligent while becoming less valuable if agency becomes increasingly concentrated.

The purpose of intelligence is participation, not replacement.
