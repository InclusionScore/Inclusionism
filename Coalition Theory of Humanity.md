# Coalition Theory of Humanity

## Conventional View

Humanity is a category.

Membership is determined by fixed characteristics.

## Inclusionist View

Humanity is a coalition.

Humanity expands whenever societies extend dignity, participation, and belonging to previously excluded people.

## Historical Pattern

Aristocrats  
→ Citizens

Men  
→ Women

Dominant Ethnic Groups  
→ Minority Groups

Heterosexuality  
→ LGBTQ+ Communities

## Principle

Humanity grows through inclusion.

Humanity contracts through exclusion.

## Inclusionist Horizon

The future of civilization depends upon expanding humanity's coalition without destroying social cohesion.
